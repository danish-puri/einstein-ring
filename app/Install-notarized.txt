EINSTEIN RING
A black hole, a sky full of stars, and one pale blue dot.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS Ventura 13 or later.
The video is included. No Terminal, Xcode, or Homebrew is needed.

1. Drag Einstein Ring into Applications.
2. Open Einstein Ring from Applications and choose Start Wallpaper.
3. Use the sparkles icon in the menu bar to pause or choose Open at Login.
4. Eject the Einstein Ring disk image.

This build is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. macOS may ask
you to confirm that you want to open an app downloaded from the internet.

TO REMOVE
Turn off Open at Login in the sparkles menu, quit, and move the app to the Trash.
Your existing desktop picture remains underneath.

HELP
Motion pauses when hidden, during display sleep, and in Low Power Mode.
The app offers to replace older wallpaper.sh installations on first launch.
Report problems at https://github.com/danish-puri/einstein-ring/issues
