EINSTEIN RING
A black hole, a sky full of stars, and one pale blue dot.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS Ventura 13 or later.
The video is included. No Terminal, Xcode, Homebrew, or internet is needed to play it.

1. Drag Einstein Ring into Applications.
2. Open Einstein Ring from Applications, then choose Start Wallpaper.
3. Use the sparkles icon in the menu bar to pause or choose Open at Login.
4. Eject the Einstein Ring disk image.

FIRST OPEN
This release is not notarized by Apple. macOS may block the first launch.
After trying to open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
to the message about Einstein Ring, and choose Open Anyway. Confirm Open.
Only approve a copy you downloaded from the project's GitHub release:
https://github.com/danish-puri/einstein-ring/releases/latest
You do not need to disable Gatekeeper or run a Terminal command.

TO REMOVE
Turn off Open at Login in the sparkles menu, quit the app, then move it from
Applications to the Trash. Your existing desktop picture remains underneath.

UPGRADING FROM WALLPAPER.SH
The app offers to replace the old player and its login item. Choose Replace,
then use Open at Login if you want the new app to start when you sign in.

HELP
The motion pauses when the desktop is fully covered, the displays sleep,
or Low Power Mode is enabled. Turn off Low Power Mode to resume motion.
If Open at Login needs approval, choose Approve Login Startup in the app's menu.
If the menu bar is crowded, close another menu bar app to reveal the sparkles.
Report problems at https://github.com/danish-puri/einstein-ring/issues
